The Mechanical Surfboard runs on Salt Water as well as Water.

[list]
[*]Pipe either one into the surfboard: no damage, nothing dumped on the floor.
[*]The board spills what it was fed, so a Salt Water board leaves Salt Water puddles.
[*]Every other liquid is still rejected, as in the base game.
[/list]

Made by a professional software developer with assistance from Claude Fable. Comes with a no slop guarantee.
