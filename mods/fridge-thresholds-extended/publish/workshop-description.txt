Makes [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2959340381]Storage Refrigerator Thresholds[/url] work with more fridges. This mod provides no content of its own, it merely adds compatibility.

[list]
[*][b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2618339179]Freezer[/url][/b]: the sliders already showed up, now they actually work.
[*][b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3557584850]Ronivan's Legacy - Industrial Revolution[/url][/b]: all five fridges get the sliders.
[*][b]Mini Fridge[/b] from The Aquatic Planet Pack.
[*][b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3756115269]Mini Freezer[/url][/b].
[*][b]Cool Pod[/b] from [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3812993268]Storage Pod Redux[/url].
[/list]

[b]Compatibility[/b]
Requires Storage Refrigerator Thresholds. Freezer, Mini Freezer, Industrial Revolution and Storage Pod Redux are all optional; whichever you don't have is skipped. Build 744825 or newer.

Made by a professional software developer with assistance from Claude Code. Comes with a no slop guarantee.
