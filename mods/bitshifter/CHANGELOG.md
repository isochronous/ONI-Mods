# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Fan-out Gate: while its input is on, sends the bits picked in its side screen on a ribbon.

## 1.3.0 - 2026-11-08

### Added

- Ribbon Diagnostic Panel: shows all 31 bits of a ribbon on a grid of lamps, green for on and red for off. With its output wired, it is also a 31-channel switch.
- Chinese (Simplified), Korean and Russian translations.

### Changed

- Works with the U60 update as well as the current game.

## 1.2.1 - 2026-10-29

- Pasting Ribbon Readers or Writers with Blueprints Expanded while Better Automation Overlay is enabled no longer crashes the game.

## 1.2.0 - 2026-10-16

- A NOT gate on a ribbon inverts all 31 bits, not just bits 1-4.
- The bit number reads the right way up on a Reader or Writer stood on end either way.
- The Ribbon Reader's build menu icon faces the same way as the original.

## 1.1.0 - 2026-10-16

- The Ribbon Reader and Ribbon Writer show the selected bit on a small display, with a light for that bit's state.
- New preview image

## 1.0.0 - 2026-10-14

- First release: the Ribbon Reader and Ribbon Writer can select bits 1-31. Stands down when Digital CPU is enabled, or when another mod already patches the bit depth.
