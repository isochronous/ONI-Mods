# Changelog

## 0.2.1 - 2026-10-12

- Korean: the "Any" choice now reads 아무 원소 (any element), the game's own phrase, instead of a bare 아무.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-10-12

- Chinese (Simplified), Korean and Russian translations. Translations load from translations/<locale>.po; the template for other languages is translations/DetectAnyElement.pot.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-12

- First version: an "Any" choice under "None" in the element sensors' side screen. The Liquid Pipe, Gas Pipe and Conveyor Rail Element Sensors go green for any packet passing through; the Gas and Liquid Element Sensors for any gas or liquid in their cell. Picking an element switches Any off again.
- A sensor on Any flashes an asterisk on its screen while it is on, in place of (and in the colour of) the drop, gas gust or lump of ore it shows for an element.
