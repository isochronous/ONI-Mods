Makes map borders perfectly smooth.

Also allows customizing the border thickness - if you for some reason want 16 tile thick walls, go nuts, but going beyond vanilla thickness may affect POI placement (they still get placed just not where they would be with vanilla settings). Different asteroid types have different default thicknesses, so you can customize each. Defaults for everything are vanilla.

[b]Compatibility[/b]
Works on build 744825 (U59) or newer. Asteroids from other worldgen mods use the setting for the kind of asteroid their border matches, or get a flat wall at their own generation's thinnest. Tested with Empty Worlds, Baator, Omnificia Asteroid II, Voidria and MiniBase.
