Lets an Automation Wire Bridge or Automation Ribbon Bridge be placed with either end directly on a building's automation port, and that port counts as connected.

In the base game a bridge only conducts into a cell that has a wire on it, so a bridge next to a sensor still needs a wire laid under its end. With this mod the end is its own piece of wire: nothing to build, nothing to deconstruct, nothing to pay for.

[h2]What it does[/h2]
[list]
[*]A bridge end can be placed on any automation port cell: a machine's input or output, a logic gate's port, or another bridge's end. The port is on the bridge's network. Bridges chain end-to-end, and several can meet at one cell.
[*]A bridge end with no wire under it is drawn as a piece of wire of the bridge's kind (wire or ribbon), coloured by its network in the automation overlay like any wire.
[*]A bridge end behaves like a wire cell: it connects only to cells you drag the wire tool between, and the disconnect tool cuts at it like at a wire.
[*]Deconstructing the bridge takes its end pieces with it; they cost nothing and yield nothing.
[*]Bridges whose ends sit on real wires work exactly as before.
[*]Overload is unchanged: a ribbon signal through a wire bridge damages the bridge as in the base game.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Works with the base game and all DLCs, on build 744825 (U59) or newer. No dependencies. A new game is not required, and removing the mod leaves plain bridges that need wires under their ends again.
