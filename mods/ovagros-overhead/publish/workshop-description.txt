Does one thing: allows Ovagro nodes to be planted on the ceiling. Dupes can plant them in flipped farm and hydroponic tiles, and pips can plant them in natural ceiling tiles. Vines will grow along the ceiling just like they grow on the floor.

If removed from a save, ceiling Ovagro and their vines simply vanish; no seed is dropped. Cancel any Ovagro orders on upside-down farm tiles first.
