Wire bridges are just bent wires.

An automation bridge, power bridge or Heavi-Watt Joint Plate (plain or conductive) can be placed with either end directly on a building's port, and that port counts as connected: the bridge end acts as its own piece of wire, drawn like one, with nothing to build, deconstruct or pay for. Bridges of one kind can be daisy-chained, end on end; a joint plate end shares a cell only with another joint plate's end, not with a wire bridge's. A wire dragged up to a bridge end joins it as it would join a wire. Bridges whose ends sit on real wires work exactly as before.

[h2]Options[/h2]
Automation bridges and power bridges can each be turned off in the mod's options. A restart applies the change.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Works with the base game and all DLCs, on build 744825 (U59) or newer. No dependencies. A new game is not required, and removing the mod leaves plain bridges that need wires under their ends again.
