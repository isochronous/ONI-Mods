Rebuild every building in an area in another material, or replace one type with another, in one box-drag. Settings are kept.

[b]Requires [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3468585385]Blueprints Expanded[/url] by Sgt_Imalas[/b], which keeps each building's settings across the upgrade.

[h2]How it works[/h2]
Dragging a pipe over an existing pipe is already a replacement in the game: the old one keeps working until the new one is built over it. This mod does that for a whole area at once. Pick what to replace and what to replace it with, then box-drag; every matching building gets a replacement order at the current priority.

[h2]Using it[/h2]
Select the Upgrade Planner from the toolbar (or Shift+U, rebindable). The window has two tabs.
[list]
[*][b]Change material[/b]: pick the material to replace (any, or one) and the one to build in, then the buildings to rebuild, as the build menu shows them. Tick [b]All buildings[/b] to offer machines, sensors, gates and everything else too: those are deconstructed and rebuilt in the new material through the game's own reconstruct, with their settings.
[*][b]Replace type[/b]: pick a type and what to replace it with (the types the game lets you build over it), each with its materials.
[*]Box-drag. Everything the plan would touch is highlighted while the tool is active, and a popup reports how many were marked.
[*]Save plans as presets, one list per tab.
[/list]

[h2]Good to know[/h2]
[list]
[*]Sidegrades and downgrades work too: sandstone to steel, insulated back to plain.
[*]Modded pipes appear automatically, since the list comes from the game's own replacement rules. Two-ingredient recipes (steel and plastic) get two material rows.
[*]Nothing the build tool would refuse is allowed, and only the buildings All buildings adds are deconstructed. Cancel orders with the Cancel tool as usual.
[*]Closing the window ends the tool; the plan is remembered for the session.
[/list]
