Critter sensors no longer count eggs that are riding a conveyor rail through the room.

[list]
[*]An egg on a rail is not counted by any critter sensor while it passes through, so automation built on the egg count stops misfiring whenever a rail crosses the room.
[*]Eggs on the floor, in incubators, in storage, or carried by duplicants count exactly as before.
[/list]

One small patch on how eggs report their room; sensors and rails are not touched.

Made by a professional software developer with assistance from Claude Fable.

Source and issues: https://github.com/isochronous/ONI-Mods
